An overfull WIP basket changes the mood of the studio. The projects stop feeling alive and start feeling accusatory, which is not a useful atmosphere for making anything well.

  label="Basket contents spread out for a reset"

## What helps me reset

I am trying to keep only a small number of active projects in motion at once, with everything else clearly labeled as planned or paused. That makes it easier to tell the difference between a real idea and a distraction.

## Current rule

If I cannot explain why a project is active right now, it probably is not active.

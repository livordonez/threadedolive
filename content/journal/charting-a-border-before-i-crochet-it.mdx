There is always a moment in a crochet project when the border tries to become its own project. Sometimes that is a good thing. More often, it is where the design loses discipline.

<InlineImage
  label="Penciled chart annotations around a border corner"
  caption="Placeholder image for the border planning notes."

## Why charting helps

Even a rough chart shows whether the edge is actually supporting the center or simply competing with it. That is much harder to see once I am already stitching.

## The rule I keep relearning

If the center already carries the texture, the border usually needs to carry the calm.

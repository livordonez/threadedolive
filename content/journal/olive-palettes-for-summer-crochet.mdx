I keep returning to olive because it behaves like a neutral without becoming bland. The challenge is not choosing olive itself. The challenge is giving it enough contrast that it feels dimensional.

  label="Small yarn wraps laid over linen and charcoal swatches"

## Pairings that keep working

- Warm cream keeps the palette breathable.
- Muted pimento acts like punctuation.
- Brass tones add warmth without pushing things into shine.
- Charcoal gives the darker olives something to push against.

## Tool note

This is one of those moments when simple paper wraps and daylight are still the best tools. I trust them more than a screen for close palette decisions.

The earliest surprise was not that needlepoint felt different from crochet. It was **how differently** it asks for attention. The pace is steadier, but the cost of drifting is higher because a small counting error can echo across the whole motif.

<InlineImage
  label="Canvas marked with a pencil note about recounting"

## What helped

Stopping every few rows to step back made a bigger difference than trying to count perfectly in one stretch. The distance check caught shape problems much earlier.

## What I want next

I want the next study to include more compensation stitches and at least one color change that forces me to pay attention to coverage, not just counting.

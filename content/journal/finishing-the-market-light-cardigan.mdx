The biggest lesson from this cardigan is that a garment can look almost finished for days while still feeling unresolved. For me, the missing piece was the finishing language: the width of the band, the way the hem sat, and the discipline of the last rows.

## What changed

I narrowed the band slightly, lengthened the sleeves by one repeat, and cleaned up the edge transitions. None of those edits sounded dramatic on paper, but together they shifted the cardigan from pleasant to intentional.

## What I am keeping in mind

Garment projects deserve a second round of design decisions at the finishing stage. The first pass gets the shape built. The second pass decides whether the piece actually feels finished.

## Why this one mattered

Garments can easily become too precious in planning and too hesitant in execution. This cardigan improved the moment I stopped trying to make it clever and started trying to make it useful.

<InlineImage
  label="Cardigan panels clipped together for the first fit check"
  caption="Fit testing placeholder before the front band was finalized."
  tone="linen"
/>

The final silhouette is deliberately quiet. The details live in the texture, the line of the shoulder, and the way the front band frames the body instead of collapsing inward.

## Construction notes

Crochet garments can feel stiff when the shaping is too abrupt, so I spread the changes over more rows than I first planned. That softened the whole piece and helped it sit more naturally once worn.

## Next time

I would like to revisit this shape in a darker palette, possibly with a denser stitch around the hem to give it slightly more weight.

## Why sewing belongs here

Sewing shifts the pace of the studio in a useful way. The decisions feel sharper and more immediate, which makes it a good counterweight to long crochet projects.

This pillow is small, but it clarified the role of pimento in the brand palette. The color works best when it behaves like punctuation rather than the whole sentence.

## Finishing details

The topstitching matters more than the patchwork itself here. It gives the piece a cleaner outline and keeps the surface from feeling floppy or unfinished.

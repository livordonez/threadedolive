## Small scale, big decisions

Embroidery compresses decision-making. Tiny changes in spacing, length, or thread weight become immediately visible, which makes it a useful craft for studying restraint.

The cuff became a kind of measuring tool for the rest of the brand. It confirmed that a quiet palette can still feel expressive when the structure is thoughtful.

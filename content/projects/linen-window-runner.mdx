## The idea

This piece sat in the space between practical and decorative for a long time. I did not want a doily-scaled object, but I also did not want something visually heavy.

<InlineImage
  label="Runner motif chart with edits in pencil"
  caption="Placeholder image for the charting stage."
  tone="brass"
/>

What finally helped was thinking about the runner as a line moving through a room. Once I paid attention to its length and rhythm instead of only the stitch detail, the rest of the decisions came faster.

## Lessons from blocking

The blocking stage completely changed the way this project read. Before that, the openwork felt uncertain. Afterward, the geometry sharpened and the negative space started doing real visual work.

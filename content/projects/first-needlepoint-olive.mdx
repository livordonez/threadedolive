## A first study

Needlepoint asks for a different kind of patience than crochet. The counting is quieter, more incremental, and more vulnerable to tiny mistakes that travel farther than expected.

<InlineImage
  label="First olive motif stitched halfway across the canvas"

/>

Using the brand olive as the subject made the project feel less like a detached exercise. Even while learning, it still belongs to the larger visual world of the studio.

## What I want to keep learning

Compensation stitches, cleaner chart notation, and how thread sheen changes the way color reads across a tiny field of stitches.

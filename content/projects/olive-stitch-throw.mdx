## Story and inspiration

This blanket began as a color exercise. I wanted to stay close to olive without letting the whole piece dissolve into one quiet middle tone, so the design depends on texture first and color second.

The finished throw has a little more gravity than the earliest sketches suggested, which ended up being the right choice. It feels closer to a textile object than a casual afghan, and that shift made the project feel more like Threaded Olive from the start.

## What I learned

When the stitch pattern is this dense, the border cannot be an afterthought. The successful version did less, not more. I removed the decorative corners, widened the plain edge, and let the central repeat remain the star.

## Process notes

I would make this again with a slightly loftier yarn if I wanted more softness, but I would keep the same structure. The tension and finish are what give the piece its sense of intention.
